About

I am an evidence-synthesis methodologist completing my doctorate (DPhil) at the Technical University of Munich, where I study how healthcare providers adopt and implement digital health and AI technologies. I have a medical background (MBBCh, Cairo University) and worked as a primary care physician in Egypt.

For more than eight years I have designed and led systematic reviews, meta-analyses, umbrella and bibliometric reviews, including evidence for the WHO Regional Office for Europe's digital-health policy work and for the German S3 guideline on menopause care. I am an author on two Cochrane reviews in progress.

I care about evidence that decision-makers can trust and use, and about helping early-career researchers do this work well.

Focus

Evidence synthesis & guidelines

Systematic reviews, overviews and scoping reviews, from protocol to publication. Meta-analysis in R, risk-of-bias assessment and GRADE certainty ratings for guideline groups and policy-makers.

🤖

Digital health & AI adoption

Why clinicians accept, resist or adapt digital tools and AI. My doctoral research compares locally and foreign-trained healthcare providers worldwide using the UTAUT2 framework.
